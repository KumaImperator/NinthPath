Ninth OATH: Classes & Races Overview

Official Game Design Reference Document — Dark Theme Edition

1. Weapon Classes & Progression Blueprint

Progression Rule: Players begin as a Commoner. Choose a weapon class first, then choose one compatible class path; paths are not separate basic classes. Every existing path keeps its own evolution and specializations.

3. Playable Races & Lineage Evolution

Humanoids

Human

Passive: Versatile base stats across all attributes; +10% overall EXP gain modifier.

Active: Focused Ambition (10-min temporary stat boost based on active class).

Advantages: Pivots flexibly to any class; grants exclusive access to the 'T.W.O' class.

Drawbacks: Lower specialized stat ceilings compared to non-humans.

Dwarves & Craftfolk

Dwarf

Passive (Subclass Dependent):
• Warrior Dwarf: +20% starting HP (+5% late-game), stance stability, bonus melee damage with shortswords/hammers.
• Craftsman Dwarf: Increased rare ore yield, native workbench/forge access, tunnel mastery, item bonuses.

Active: Stoneform (30% Physical Damage reduction & knockback immunity for 8s).

Advantages: Specialized combat efficiency with hammers/shortswords OR supreme smithing/forging.

Drawbacks: Reduced combat efficiency when wielding Greatswords, Staves, Bows, and Polearms.

Elves & Naturekin

Elf

Passive: +10% Max Mana, accelerated nature-element casting, +15% HP regen.

Active: Arcane Burst (Restores 25% Max Mana instantly; 3m cooldown).

Advantages: High effectiveness with bows, crossbows, and nature magic.

Drawbacks: Lower physical Stamina/HP; debuffed when nature is damaged.

Active: Root Entangle (Immobilizes surrounding targets).

Advantages: Extreme tankiness and natural sustain in woodland regions.

Drawbacks: Takes +50% increased damage from Fire elemental sources.
